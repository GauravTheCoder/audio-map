    Sound guide

    • Two low falling notes: the road ends ahead (dead end).
    • Quickening beeps: an intersection is coming up; they speed up and rise as you get closer, then the bell plays when you are in it. Lower beeps mean a dead end.
    • Rising chime: intersection ahead, one note per road. Ticking after it: traffic signals.
    • Ding-dong bell: you are at the intersection now, the moment to turn. Short pings follow from the direction of each road you can take, left to right; the highest ping is straight ahead. A soft falling blip means you have left the intersection.
    • Rumble on one side: you are drifting toward that edge of the road. Off road, the rumble comes from where the road is.
    • Click while turning: every 10 degrees. Double chime: north, east, south or west.
    • Place sounds come from the direction of the place: cup clink for food, two-tone chime for transport, bell for culture, water drop for parks, plain tone for shops and services.
    • Beacon ping: your destination. Left or right in your headphones shows which side it is on; pitch shows ahead (high) or behind (low). A second, higher note means you are heading straight at it. It repeats faster as you get closer. With turn-by-turn on, the beacon points a little way along the route, so following it follows the streets.
    • Following a route: at the intersection where you turn, a bright rising chord plays from the side to turn to, and you hear "Here: turn left onto…". Intersections you go straight through get a single soft bell instead of the ding-dong. Earlier reminders count the intersections, e.g. "at the second intersection".
    • Low falling pair from behind you, with "Wrong way. Turn around.": you are travelling back along the route. When the route is behind you, I starts with "Turn around" and N ends with "Your route is behind you".
    • "Map data ends": the road goes on, but that part of the map has not loaded yet. It loads as you get closer; it is not a dead end.
    • Rising two notes on one side: a turn that is not at an intersection (or a bend, with road assist off) is starting now, toward that side.
    • Bends: the road's bends are announced ahead with their direction and size, e.g. "Road bends right in 40 metres". Walkers also hear the angle ("about 60 degrees"), as they do for each road at an intersection. With road assist off, the rising two notes play on that side as the bend begins.
    • Roundabouts: on the approach you hear every exit in order and where it leads ("second North Road, straight on"). Inside, each exit is called out as it comes up ("Exit 2, North Road, on your right") and the ding-dong bell plays at it, with a ping from the exit's direction: that is the moment to turn off.
